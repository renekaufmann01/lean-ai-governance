EU AI Act: Risk-Based Framework

The world’s first comprehensive AI law. AI systems are categorized by the risk they pose to society.

UNACCEPTABLE RISK Banned

Cognitive manipulation, social scoring, or real-time remote biometric ID in public spaces.

HIGH RISK Strictly Regulated

AI in recruitment, medical devices, critical infrastructure, or law enforcement. Requires risk audits and human oversight.

LIMITED RISK Transparency Rules

Chatbots and deepfakes. Users must be informed they are interacting with an AI.

MINIMAL RISK No New Obligations

Spam filters, AI-enabled video games, or inventory management systems.

The Penalty for Non-Compliance: Fines up to €35 Million or 7% of global annual turnover.
